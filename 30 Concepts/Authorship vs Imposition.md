# Authorship vs Imposition

## Definition (as held)
A norm **authored** by those it governs can be revised by them; a norm **imposed** can only be obeyed or broken. The same rule is additive when authored and displacing when imposed — so the *source* of a norm changes its dose.

## Why it matters to sovereignty
This is the bridge between [[Normalisation]] and [[Sovereignty]]: standardisation is domination *when imposed*. It also answers a blank in [[Agency without Domination]] — not all standardising is domination.
