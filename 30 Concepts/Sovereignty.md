# Sovereignty

## Definition (as held)
Authority over one's own thinking, works, and systems — claimed *absolutely* ([[Agency without Domination]]) — and **legitimate only when it preserves others' sovereignty**.

## The two halves
1. **Self**: not being standardised, controlled, or overridden.
2. **Other**: the refusal to do any of those to anyone else. "The rejection of being controlled must never invert into the impulse to control."

The second half is where the framework stops being libertarian: sovereignty is *conditional* on reciprocity, which makes reciprocity — possibly — the meaning of "Symmetric". → [[OQ-11 The Name]]
