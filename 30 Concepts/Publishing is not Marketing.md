# Publishing is not Marketing

## Definition (as held)
The long-term content asset is a **durable catalogue of public thinking**. The mechanism is *recognition*, not persuasion. The time signature is *years*, not quarters.

## Why it matters
Standard marketing tactics were found incoherent with the philosophy; this reframe is what makes public writing coherent — it is the philosophy in public, not a funnel. → [[Outreach and Content]], [[Thread — Outreach Avoidance]]
