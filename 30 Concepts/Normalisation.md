# Normalisation

## Definition (as held)
The process by which modern society, culture, and business **flatten individuality and erode genuine human tension**. The notebook's image: "the standardised stillness sold as peace" — "a smoother, emptier version" of the human anomaly.

## Reframed as dosage
Not an inversion. **Progressive displacement.** A norm is a force; in small doses it adds; the damage is when it displaces other forces entirely. → [[Dosage]], [[Additive vs Displacing Forces]]

## What determines the dose
- Who wrote the norm → [[Authorship vs Imposition]]
- Whether the system shares capability or goods → [[Capability-sharing vs Goods-sharing]]

## Relationship to tension
Normalisation *suppresses* tension; the framework does not create tension, it stops hiding it. → [[Tension]]

## Named in the values
[[Agency without Domination]] lists *standardise* alongside control and override. [[Optimisation without Suppression]] is the anti-normalisation value.
