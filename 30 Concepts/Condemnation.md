# Condemnation

## Definition (as held)
The shadow of [[Kindness]]. Kindness that has decided who deserves it.

## Why this and not weakness
The usual charge against kindness is that it goes soft. The framework instead says it goes *moral* — it acquires a standard, and a standard is a norm, and a norm at the wrong dose is [[Normalisation]]. Condemnation is also irreversible in a way kindness never is: the condemned have no exit → [[Reversibility and Exit]].
