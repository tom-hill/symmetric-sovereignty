# Exit Cost

## The proposal
Sovereignty cannot be measured, and [[OQ-17 Can Sovereignty Be Netted]] argues it should not be. But **what it costs a particular person to leave** can be observed without building a sovereignty metric. Exit cost is a proxy for power imbalance that is procedural rather than consequentialist.

| Case | Exit cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Local shared pot, village scale | Low — move, or decline to use it | Legitimate |
| Mandatory schooling, 1880 | High, and the alternative was the trap it was built against | Contested, and later reduced by home-schooling provision |
| National taxation | High — emigration | Tom: illegitimate at current scale. → [[OQ-18 The Unit of Revisability]] |
| Selling yourself into slavery | Infinite, by construction | The limit case, and why it is the limit case |
| Tribute under occupation | Infinite — the occupier follows | Illegitimate on all three tests |

## Why it may be the operationalisation the framework needs
It answers the epistemic objection in [[OQ-17 Can Sovereignty Be Netted]] — you cannot demonstrate that a trade protected sovereignty, but you can observe what leaving costs — without building the single scalar that [[OQ-08 Why the Obsession]] and [[Optimisation without Suppression]] both warn against. It also explains why local norms have felt different throughout this argument: not because they are small, but because leaving them is cheap.

## The sharpest consequence
**The same norm has different exit costs for different people.** A flat regime is exitable for the wealthy and foreclosing for the poor — identical rule, different foreclosure. So foreclosure is not a property of a norm at all. It is a property of *a norm and a person together*.

> [!claude]
> If that holds it does real damage in a useful direction. [[Foreclosure]] stops being a test you can run on a policy and becomes a distribution across everyone subject to it — which is [[Variance and the Average That Doesn't Exist]] arriving from a third direction, and it means any legitimacy claim made about a norm in general is a claim about its average subject, who does not exist.

## Open
Exit cost is still not a single number, varies by person, and may smuggle the scalar back in through the side door. Treat as a proposal under test.
