# Tension

## Definition (as held)
The automatic result of real forces acting on a real system. **Neutral physics** — not created by the framework, only *revealed* by it, because normalisation suppresses it. Tension is the generative condition: where there is none, there is "no individuality, creativity, originality, or joy" (notebook).

## Not conflict
Resolution produces imbalance; the aim is to hold tension consciously, not dissolve it. (But see [[OQ-12 Tension vs Conflict]] — the notebook includes conflict.)

## Not balance
"Balance" and "symmetry" are [[Resolution Words]]; they imply a rest state. There is none. → [[OQ-11 The Name]]
