# Genuine Wants

## Definition (as held)
**Genuine wants require no self-persuasion.** Used as a reliable epistemological instrument: any goal that needed active self-reasoning to adopt was probably never genuinely Tom's.

## Applications
- Diagnosing the avoidance of outreach → [[Thread — Outreach Avoidance]]
- The "bridge" status of the fractional practice → [[Part 9 — The Professional]]
- A test for whether a value has been *held* or *adopted*
