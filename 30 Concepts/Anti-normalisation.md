# Anti-normalisation

## Definition (as held)
The critical stance of the framework: that the dominant forces of society, culture, and business flatten individuality, and that this flattening — not struggle, not tension — is the deeper danger.

## Its origin in the material
Part of the framework's founding claim that *minds that don't fit standard categories are not broken* ([[Part 0 — Why This Exists]]).
