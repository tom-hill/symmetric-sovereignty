# Struggle Revealed Through Experience

## Definition (as held)
Struggle reveals itself through **experience, not stated intention**. Frameworks based on observed behaviour are preferred over self-report — particularly in developmental contexts.

## Applications
- The Reyor practice: diagnosis from what an organisation *does* under a discontinuity
- The philosophy's own method: [[Inductive Method]]
- [[Vulnerability without Collapse]]: where the line between transparency and collapse actually is, found empirically
