# Dosage

## Definition (as held)
The framing under which a force is judged not by whether it is *good or bad* but by whether it **adds without displacing** or **displaces entirely**. Normalisation is a dosage problem.

## Consequences
- Turns many either/or questions into how-much questions — including, possibly, the VC critique ([[OQ-03 VC Critique — Mechanism or Scale]]).
- Introduces a second failure physics alongside inversion → [[OQ-01 Inversion vs Dosage Risks]].
- Raises the question of a dose-response curve: threshold, gradient, or cliff? (blank in [[Part 6 — Normalisation as Dosage]]).
