# Kindness

## Definition (as held)
The **mechanism** that prevents powerful values from inverting into their shadows. Not a value in the "X without Y" set — a guard on all of them.

## Its own shadow
[[Condemnation]]. → [[OQ-10 Condemnation as Kindness's Shadow]]

## How it might work (not yet argued)
> [!claude]
> Each shadow is what a value becomes when it stops seeing the other person as sovereign. Kindness, read structurally, is *the continued perception of the other as sovereign under pressure* — not softness. That would make it the operational form of "sovereignty is legitimate only when it preserves others' sovereignty", and would explain why its shadow is condemnation (the moment you stop seeing a sovereign and start seeing a case).
