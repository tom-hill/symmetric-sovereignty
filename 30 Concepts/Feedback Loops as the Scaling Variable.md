# Feedback Loops as the Scaling Variable

## Definition (as held)
What changes as a moral principle scales is not the **principle** but the **feedback loops**. Individual morality is disciplined by direct consequence — you see the person you harm. Collective morality operates with diffuse causality, long time horizons, and diffused responsibility.

Institutions, laws and norms exist to **simulate at scale what direct consequence does naturally at the individual level**. They are imperfect translations, and the imperfection is where moral paradox at scale comes from.

## The worked paradoxes
| At the individual scale | Extended upward |
|---|---|
| Honesty is a virtue | Total state transparency destabilises systems and gets people killed |
| Loyalty to family is moral | Extended unconditionally to nation, it becomes tribalism |
| Self-sacrifice for a child is heroic | Self-sacrifice for an abstraction produces terrorism |

## Why it matters
It is the mechanism [[Part 7 — Scales]] currently lacks. The part asserts that the principle holds across scales; this says *what has to be rebuilt* at each scale for it to keep holding, and why the duty layer is the part that scales — a duty is machinery, and machinery is how you simulate a feedback loop. → [[Restraint in Machinery]], [[Duties are Invoked]]
