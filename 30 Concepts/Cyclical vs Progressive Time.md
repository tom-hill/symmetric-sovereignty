# Cyclical vs Progressive Time

## Definition (as held)
Whether tension is assumed to produce **forward movement** (growth, emergence, advancement) or **return** (restoration, maintenance, cycle). The notebook assumes the first throughout. That assumption is post-Enlightenment and Western; most older civilisations operate on cyclical time, where the resolution of conflict is restoration of balance rather than innovation.

## The challenge it poses
> [!claude]
> A philosophy oriented around return and maintenance would look at this framework and ask: *why do you assume the tension should go somewhere?* That is not a rhetorical question. "Health is consciously held tension" is compatible with cyclical time. "Tension is generative" — generative *of* what, arriving *where* — may not be.

If the answer is that tension generates novelty and development, the framework has quietly adopted a progress metaphysics it never argued for, and [[Part 1 — Tension as the Generative Condition]] needs to say so and defend it.
