# Convergence as Evidence

## Definition (as held)
When a principle arrives **independently from multiple directions** in the course of the work, that convergence is treated as evidence that the principle is structurally load-bearing rather than decorative.

## Used for
[[Reversibility and Exit]] — five arrivals.

## Caution
> [!claude]
> Convergence in a single mind, in dialogue with a single partner, can be an echo. The test is stronger when the directions are genuinely independent (a notebook page written before the dialogue; an external author; an empirical observation from practice). Worth stating the standard so the method can't be gamed by enthusiasm.
