# Layer not Scale

## Definition (as held)
Whether a shared thing adds or displaces is a question of **layer**, not of size or even of addition. A norm that *occupies the slot* another practice held displaces it. A norm that *sits beneath the slot* enables — local practice can be rebuilt on top of it.

The worked case: germ theory does not compete with local medicine for the same position. It sits underneath, and local practice survives and improves on top. A standardised diet, by contrast, occupies the slot the local diet held.

## Why it matters
It gives [[Additive vs Displacing Forces]] a mechanism it previously lacked. "Does this add or displace?" was a question with no test; "does this sit beneath the slot or occupy it?" is answerable about a specific norm. → [[OQ-14 Normalisation as the Only Unheld Tension]]

## The unresolved objection
> [!claude]
> Network effects break the layer test at the boundary. Access to English sits beneath everything — and kills the language underneath anyway, because the value of a norm rises with the number already holding it. Additive at the individual level and subtractive at the system level are **the same event viewed from two distances**. So the test needs a timescale and a level attached, or it will approve things that hollow out.
