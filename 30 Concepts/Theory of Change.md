# Theory of Change

## Definition (as held — Tom's own)
Change spreads by **behavioural contagion across generations**, not by argument or campaign.

> [!question] Tom
> I can't control masses. I can't force people not to incentivise for themselves. I can show people a better path, and help them follow it. If people following the better path do better as a result, if it lifts them up, then people are more likely to follow it. This is how things like racism and sexism have changed. […] 1 grandparent who stood up to racism leads to 2 children who do, to 4 grandchildren. And so the effect spreads. With time, the majority shift. Not with force. Not with incentive.

Two riders Tom attaches: **"we don't need 100% adoption to consider victory"**, and the mechanism is shifting *an influential minority* gradually, because the majority follow the majority.

## The time horizon
> [!question] Tom
> The change I hope to see will 100% likely not happen in my lifetime. If I'm lucky, my grandchildren may live to see it. But I don't seek "fame"… I seek to have an outsized impact on society. That doesn't mean I don't seek to do well for myself now, to help my family, to uplift myself. But I intend to do that through a mechanism that can scale to do the same for others.

## Consequences for the work
- The manuscript is not the mechanism. **Demonstration is** — the philosophy needs to be lived legibly more than argued well. → [[OQ-02 Creed to Prescription]], [[Part 9 — The Professional]]
- It makes [[Publishing is not Marketing]] structural rather than stylistic: recognition over persuasion is not a preference, it is what behavioural contagion requires.

> [!claude]
> Two strains worth keeping visible. First, contagion works whether or not people have a latent cooperative instinct — it only requires that following the path visibly pays. So this is an incentive argument, which sits awkwardly beside "not with force, not with incentive". Second, it requires the life to be *visible*, which is in tension with the aversion to self-promotion diagnosed in [[Thread — Outreach Avoidance]]. The theory of change needs the outreach the temperament resists. → [[OQ-15 The Hidden Premise About Human Nature]]
