# Foreclosure

## Definition (as held)
The condition in which a norm can no longer be corrected by the people it is wrong for, because there is no longer an outside. A local norm is **exitable** (you can walk to the next valley), **revisable** (you can argue with the people who wrote it), and carries a **feedback path** (its authors live with its consequences). A norm that has gone global has removed all three.

## Why it matters
It displaced "scale" as the criterion for what makes [[Normalisation]] harmful. The enemy is not normalisation at scale; it is **normalisation that has closed the exit**. Size only correlates because past a certain size nobody living under a norm can have written it or leave it. → [[OQ-14 Normalisation as the Only Unheld Tension]]

This makes the anti-normalisation argument testable rather than a matter of taste, and it is a fifth arrival of [[Reversibility and Exit]] from a direction the vault had not recorded.
