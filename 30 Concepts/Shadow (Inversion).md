# Shadow (Inversion)

## Definition (as held)
Each value "X without Y" names a strength X and its **shadow** Y — what X becomes when it stops holding its tension. Cruelty is strength's shadow; domination is agency's; entitlement is leadership's; collapse, rigidity, suppression, paralysis for the rest. [[Condemnation]] is the shadow of [[Kindness]] itself.

## Inversion
The notebook's word: "the rejection of being controlled must never *invert* into the impulse to control." An inversion is a flip, not a drift.

## Two physics?
The dialogue later described [[Normalisation]] as a [[Dosage]] problem — progressive displacement, *not* inversion. Whether the values also fail by dosage, and which, is [[OQ-01 Inversion vs Dosage Risks]].

## What drives the flip?
Not yet argued. Candidate: **fear** → [[OQ-06 Fear]]. Candidate: loss of the perception of the other as sovereign → [[Kindness]].
