# Values vs Duties

## The two layers
| | Values | Duties |
|---|---|---|
| Form | "X without Y" tensions | obligations |
| Held by | the self | owed to a counterparty |
| Administered | self-administered — nobody can hold it for you | binding — including on those who reject the philosophy |
| Together form | a personal creed | a "philosophical constitution" |

## Why the split matters
- Values cannot be prescribed (they are held, not obeyed) — which is why the framework needed a second layer once it stopped being "just about me". → [[OQ-02 Creed to Prescription]]
- Duties are what let the principle *scale*: a company or a state cannot hold a value, but it can be bound by a duty. → [[Scales]]
- Some notebook values contain a duty inside them: [[Vulnerability without Collapse]] speaks of "those who depend on you".

## Open

## Working definitions (proposed)
> [!claude]
> A **value** is a tension held by the self. A **principle** is a way of holding it (see [[Proactive Reactivity]]). A **duty** is a tension owed to another.
