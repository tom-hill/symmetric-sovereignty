# Attribution not Blame

## Definition (as held)
**Naming the cause and owning the response are both required.** Stating a fact about what was done to you is not an excuse; owning your response does not require pretending nothing was done. The principle is bidirectional — drop the first half and it becomes hustle culture ("everything is your responsibility"), drop the second and it becomes grievance.

## Why it matters
It may be the duty that makes the others operable. A duty that cannot be invoked without the invoker being told they are making excuses is not invocable in practice — so [[Duties are Invoked]] depends on attribution being legitimate. → [[OQ-13 Enumerate the Duties]]

It also bears on [[Condemnation]]: condemnation is what blame becomes when attribution is refused, and the person becomes a case rather than a cause with a response.
