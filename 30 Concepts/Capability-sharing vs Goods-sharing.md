# Capability-sharing vs Goods-sharing

## Definition (as held)
Sharing **capability** (teaching, equipping, opening) creates a positive flywheel — each recipient becomes a source. Sharing **goods** (handing over the finished thing) risks dependency. A structural distinction within the framework.

## Where it bites
- [[Strength without Cruelty]] — "elevate the strength of others" means elevate their *capability*
- Dependency is the quietest [[Normalisation]]: the recipient's own force is displaced by the gift
- The VC model hands over goods (capital) and generates dependency on the next round → [[Part 8 — The Model for Modern Business is Broken]]
- Reyor's differentiator — change that *outlasts his tenure* — is capability-sharing by design → [[Reyor]]
