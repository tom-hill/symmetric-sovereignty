# Scales

## Definition (as held)
The core principle extends across four scales: **individual, relational, institutional, civilisational**. What is healthy at each is consciously held tension.

## What changes with scale (to be argued)
- Individual: holding is an act of attention; values suffice.
- Relational: needs a second sovereign's consent; duties begin.
- Institutional: needs structure — the very thing that normalises. → [[Optimisation without Suppression]]
- Civilisational: becomes politics and economics. → [[Part 8 — The Model for Modern Business is Broken]]
