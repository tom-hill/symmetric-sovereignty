# Inductive Method

## Definition (as held)
The philosophy is built **upward from lived experience**, not downward from theory. Notebook first; dialogue to excavate and refine; the manuscript last.

## Why
- Process philosophy: a process cannot be understood by stopping it → [[Process Philosophy]]
- Struggle reveals itself through experience, not stated intention → [[Struggle Revealed Through Experience]]
- The origin — one life that didn't fit — makes induction the honest method and also raises [[OQ-02 Creed to Prescription]]
