# Resolution Words

## Definition (as held)
Terms that smuggle in a **rest state** — a point at which the work of holding tension is finished. "Balance", "symmetry", "harmony", "equilibrium". Rejected because they are inconsistent with the core claim that health is *consciously held* tension, not resolved tension. "Tension" is preferred because it is neutral physics.
