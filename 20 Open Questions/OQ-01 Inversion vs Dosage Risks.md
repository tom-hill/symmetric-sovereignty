# OQ-01 — Inversion risks vs dosage risks in "X without Y"

## The question
Do the values fail by **inversion** (X flips into Y — strength becomes cruelty) or by **dosage** (X gradually becomes Y — optimisation slides into suppression)? And if both, are these *different kinds of risk* that the single "X without Y" grammar currently hides?

## Why it matters
- It decides whether Part 2 needs a second axis for each value (failure mode: flip / drift).
- It connects Part 3 (shadows, inversion) to Part 6 (normalisation, dosage) — currently they use different physics.
- It affects the *duties*: a duty against inversion is a bright line; a duty against dosage is a gradient, which is harder to make binding.

## Origin of the question

This was **question 2 of the five** put in the session recorded at [[Thread — Five Challenges Session]], phrased then as:

> [!claude]
> Some Y's are inversions: Agency without Domination is about the rejection of control becoming the impulse to control — same energy, flipped polarity. Others are just excess: Reflection without Paralysis is the same substance at the wrong dose. Nothing has inverted, you've simply overdone it. Those are two different failure mechanisms, and a reader will feel the mismatch without being able to name it.
>
> And a rider that has not yet been taken up anywhere: **your marginal note asking whether Strength/Agency/Leadership are three faces of one value might be the wrong cut.** The real sorting might be inversion-risks in one group, dosage-risks in another.

That rider matters, because it puts this question and [[OQ-05 Three Faces or One Value]] in direct competition: they propose two different partitions of the same set, and only one can be the primary one.

> [!claude]
> Hypothesis to test: the *relational* values (strength, agency — where Y is done *to someone*) fail by inversion, and the *self-directed* values fail by dosage. If that holds, the grammar could be: relational values need a **kindness** guard (Part 3), self-directed values need a **dosage** guard (Part 6). Which would tidy the book considerably — perhaps too considerably; Tom corrects overly clean binaries.

## Worked 2026-10-28 — and the answer may be three, not two

→ [[Session — 2026-10-28 — Inversion, Dosage, and a Third Thing]]

Tom's finding: both modes are real, and **more values fail by inversion than by dosage**, because it is the *application* of a value that inverts it rather than the *quantity*.

| Value | Tom's verdict | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| [[Leadership without Entitlement]] | inversion | you can be entitled without leading at all |
| [[Vulnerability without Exemption]] | scale, appearing as inversion | the weight exists whether or not it is shared |
| [[Commitment without Rigidity]] | inversion | rigidity comes from refusing new information, not from commitment |
| [[Optimisation without Suppression]] | inversion | the narrowing of what is optimised for, not the amount of optimising |
| [[Reflection without Paralysis]] | true dosage | genuinely volumetric, with individual thresholds |

### The third mechanism his arguments produce

> [!claude]
> In Commitment and Optimisation the cause is neither a flip nor an accumulation. It is **X plus a separate second thing**: commitment *plus closure*, optimisation *plus a narrowed objective*. The value keeps working throughout — a rigid person is still committed — so X has not stopped doing its job, and Tom explicitly rules out quantity. The value is inert and something else does the damage.
>
> | Mode | Y appears when | Example |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Inversion** | X's purpose is withdrawn — an *absence* | leadership that stops serving the led |
> | **Compound** | a second force is added — a *presence* | commitment plus closure |
> | **Dosage** | X accumulates past a threshold — a *quantity* | reflection into paralysis |
>
> **This changes what the duty layer can do.** This note already states that a duty against inversion is a bright line and a duty against dosage is a gradient that is hard to bind. A compound failure offers a third and better route: **regulate the catalyst, not the value.** Do not limit commitment, require openness. Do not limit optimisation, require breadth of objective. Both are invocable by a counterparty and enforceable without measuring anything — which matters, because [[OQ-17 Can Sovereignty Be Netted]] concluded no quantity in this framework can be measured. → [[Duties are Invoked]]
>
> **The objection that decides it:** if a second factor is always available, the category eats everything, since strength-minus-service also "has a catalyst". The distinction is *absence against presence*. Inversion subtracts the thing that made X a value; compound adds something that was not there. If that will not hold under pressure, the three-way collapses to two and Tom's original answer stands.

## Resolution — 2026-10-28

**Neither. The question contained a false premise, which was that X is the thing being valued.**

X is a **capacity**, and capacities are morally neutral: strength in any volume does not create cruelty, agency in any volume does not create domination, optimisation in any volume does not suppress. The value lives in the **direction of application**, which is a term the "X without Y" grammar never names. The formula reads as though X were the good thing and Y were X gone wrong; in fact X is inert and the entire moral content sits in a variable the formula omits.

So values do not fail by inversion, and they do not fail by dosage. **They fail by misdirection**, with one exception that turns out to be the important part.

| Value | Capacity | Direction that must hold | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Strength without Cruelty]] | strength | toward others | misdirection |
| [[Agency without Domination]] | sovereignty over self | not into others | misdirection |
| [[Leadership without Entitlement]] | guidance of systems | purpose beyond self | misdirection |
| [[Optimisation without Suppression]] | systematising | breadth of objective | misdirection |
| [[Commitment without Rigidity]] | dedication | openness to new information | misdirection |
| [[Vulnerability without Exemption]] | radical transparency | offered as information, not as exemption | misdirection |
| [[Reflection without Paralysis]] | inquiry | **none available** | **volume** |

Two kinds of direction, kept distinct because they behave differently: **other-regarding** (strength, agency, leadership) and **reality-regarding** (commitment, optimisation), where closure and narrowing are refusals of the world rather than of people.

### The exception is the result

[[Reflection without Paralysis]] cannot be misdirected, because you cannot reflect *at* anyone. Its failure is purely volumetric — and that single difference yields the rule the framework has been missing:

> **A misdirection is observable by the counterparty. A volume is not.**

Someone can tell you that you used strength to dominate them, narrowed an objective at their cost, or refused information they gave you. Nobody can tell you that you have reflected too much, because the threshold is internal and individual.

Which sorts the two layers by mechanism rather than by assertion:
- **Directed failures can become duties.** A counterparty has standing to invoke them. → [[Duties are Invoked]]
- **Volumetric failures can only ever be values.** Self-administered by necessity, not by preference.

→ Written into [[Part 2 — The Values]] and [[Part 4 — Duties, the Philosophical Constitution]]. Resolves [[OQ-05 Three Faces or One Value]] as a consequence.

### The residue, closed 2026-10-28

[[Vulnerability without Exemption]] briefly appeared to fail both tests and was flagged as possibly malformed. **It is not.** The word *collapse* carries two readings and the analysis had taken the wrong one.

- **Collapse as structural failure.** On this reading transparency *reduces* the risk, by letting help in. True, but it describes why the capacity is worth having, not how it fails.
- **Collapse as exemption.** Admitting a weakness and then using it to escape responsibility: *I'm not good with words.* This is what Tom meant, and the notebook definition already said so — *"preserving the structural integrity needed to honour your commitments to those who depend on you."*

On the second reading it is a clean misdirection, and the **clearest case in the set**: the same disclosure either opens you to help or closes you to responsibility, with nothing in the words to separate them. Capacity: transparency. Direction: information against exemption. Observable by: the person owed the commitment. → [[Attribution not Blame]]

The table above is corrected accordingly. No residue remains; the only open item is whether *collapse* is the right word for a failure that is really exemption.
