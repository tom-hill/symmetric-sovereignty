# OQ-09 — Are "Pace without Purpose" and "Scale without Structure" values, shadows, or errors?

## The question
The project memory lists these two among the "X without Y" value formulations. Neither appears in the photographed notebook spreads, and both read — literally — as failure modes, not values.

## Possibilities
1. **Transcription drift** in the memory: the real formulations were different (e.g. "Pace without Frenzy", "Structure without Bureaucracy").
2. **Anti-values**: Tom deliberately wrote some entries as things to avoid, and Part 2 needs an anti-values section.
3. **Values in an unphotographed spread** with a meaning that isn't obvious from the four words.

## Why it matters
Small in itself; but if (2), it changes the grammar of Part 2 — the set would contain both *tensions to hold* and *collapses to avoid*, and those are different kinds of statement.
