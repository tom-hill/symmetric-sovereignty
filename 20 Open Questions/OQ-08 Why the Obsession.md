# OQ-08 — Why the obsession with the most valuable business, the richest person?

> [!question] Tom, in the margin of the business page
> Short-termism / profit driven / vanity driven → The obsession with most valuable business? richest person? **Why?**

## Angles from the framework
- **Normalisation**: valuation is a single scalar, and a single scalar is the ultimate flattening — it lets every business be compared on one axis and every founder be ranked. The obsession may be *what a normalised culture measures because it has flattened everything else*. → [[Normalisation]]
- **Resolution word**: "most valuable" resolves the tension between the many things a business is for into one number. → [[Resolution Words]]
- **Sovereignty**: wealth is one route to not being controlled — the obsession may be a distorted form of [[Agency without Domination]] that has inverted (from *not being controlled* to *controlling*).
- **Fear**: → [[OQ-06 Fear]]. Ranking is what people do when they are afraid of not counting.
