# OQ-06 — Fear

> [!question] Tom, beside Vulnerability / Commitment / Optimisation
> Feels like there is more to this. **Fear!** How does fear play into this. Do we fear things or is fear the thing to be afraid of?

## Why it might be the missing piece
Each shadow on that spread is a fear response:
- **Collapse** is what you fear if you are transparent — so you hide (the shadow of hiding is a different collapse).
- **Rigidity** is gripping — a fear of losing the thing.
- **Suppression** is smoothing away the anomaly — a fear of the chaotic.

If that holds, fear may be the *engine of inversion* — the thing that turns X into Y — and [[Kindness]] would be its counter-mechanism. That would give Part 3 its missing "how".

## Tom's second question
*Do we fear things, or is fear the thing to be afraid of?* — a Stoic question ([[Marcus Aurelius]]), and a process one: fear of an outcome vs fear as a state that distorts the process regardless of outcome.
