# OQ-05 — Are Strength, Agency and Leadership one value?

> [!question] Tom, beside the first three values
> Is there actually a differentiation between these 3, or are they all different faces of the same value??

## The three
- [[Strength without Cruelty]] — power over *capability*, turned to protect others
- [[Agency without Domination]] — power over *self*, refusing to reach into others
- [[Leadership without Entitlement]] — power over *systems*, without superiority

## Angles
- They share a structure: a form of **power** (X) and the shadow of **using it on others** (Y). The candidate single value is something like *power without control*.
- They differ by **scale**: self / other / system — which is the [[Scales]] framework already present inside Part 2.
- Keeping them separate has a cost (redundancy) and a benefit (each shadow is distinct and recognisable: cruelty, domination, entitlement are different experiences for the person on the receiving end).

> [!claude]
> A test: can you violate one without violating the others? Entitlement without cruelty is common (the benign but superior leader). Cruelty without entitlement is possible (the cruel peer). That suggests three values with a shared root, not one — but the root should be named in Part 2.
