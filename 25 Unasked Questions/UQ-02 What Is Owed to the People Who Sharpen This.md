# UQ-02 — What is owed to the people whose pushback sharpens this?

## The sense
Someone objects. The objection is good. It changes the philosophy. It gets written into a Book part, and eventually published under Tom's name.

What do they get? Attribution? Consent before their words are used? A share of something? Nothing, because that is how ideas work?

## Why it will not stay abstract
`75 Encounters` exists precisely to capture other people's words, and the whole point of testing publicly is that strangers will supply objections Tom could not generate alone. The first good one arrives and the question is immediate.

## What it sits next to
[[Duties are Invoked]] — if there is a duty here, who can invoke it and how? · [[Capability-sharing vs Goods-sharing]] · [[Attribution not Blame]] — the name is suggestive; attribution is already a principle in this framework, just not about ideas · [[Sovereignty]]

## The uncomfortable version
The vault already contains an answer it has not noticed. Every `> [!claude]` callout is a machine's contribution, marked and attributable, on the principle that contributions should never merge silently into Tom's text. If that principle is right for a machine, it is hard to argue it is not right for a person.
