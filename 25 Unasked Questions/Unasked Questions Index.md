# Unasked Questions Index

*Questions that are still a **sense** rather than a question.*

[[Open Questions Index|20 Open Questions]] holds questions that have been formulated — they have a shape, a why-it-matters, and angles. This folder holds what comes before that: the itch, the thing noticed in passing, the place where the argument feels thin without it yet being clear why.

The reason to keep them separate is not tidiness. Forcing a half-formed question into `OQ` shape before it is ready is a small [[Normalisation]] — it makes the question legible at the cost of what was interesting about it. A `UQ` is allowed to be badly worded, embarrassing, or possibly not a question at all.

**These are published.** Tom's commitment is to publish the questions he is pondering *and the ones he has not yet asked*. This folder is the second half of that, and it promotes to public by default.

## Statuses
- **sensed** — noticed, written down, barely articulate. Most live here and that is fine.
- **forming** — starting to have a shape; a why-it-matters is emerging.
- **promoted** — became an `OQ`. Record which one and leave the note; the route from sense to question is part of the history.
