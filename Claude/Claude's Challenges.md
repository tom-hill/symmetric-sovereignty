## Structural challenges to the framework
1. **Two failure physics.** Values seem to fail by inversion (strength → cruelty) *and* by dosage (optimisation → suppression). The "X without Y" grammar hides this. Hypothesis: relational values invert; self-directed values drift. → [[OQ-01 Inversion vs Dosage Risks]]
2. **The name.** The framework rejects "symmetry" as a resolution word and is called Symmetric Sovereignty. Two rescues offered: reciprocity, or scale-invariance. → [[OQ-11 The Name]]
3. **Tension vs conflict.** The notebook includes conflict; the dialogue excludes it. → [[OQ-12 Tension vs Conflict]]
4. **No duties listed.** The constitution has no articles. Six candidates offered. → [[OQ-13 Enumerate the Duties]]
5. **"Binding on non-believers" is unargued.** Asymmetric reading (it binds *me* in how I treat *them*) is offered as the cleaner route. → [[Part 4 — Duties, the Philosophical Constitution]]
6. **Kindness needs a mechanism.** Proposed: kindness = the continued perception of the other as sovereign under pressure; its shadow, condemnation, is the moment that perception is replaced by a case. → [[Kindness]], [[OQ-10 Condemnation as Kindness's Shadow]]
7. **Fear as the engine of inversion.** Tom's margin question may be Part 3's missing "how". → [[OQ-06 Fear]]
8. **"Pace without Purpose" / "Scale without Structure"** read as failures, not values. → [[OQ-09 Pace and Scale Formulations]]
9. **Convergence can be an echo.** State the independence standard. → [[Convergence as Evidence]]
10. **The invitation escape hatch.** "It's just a report from one life" is comfortable; the VC critique already prescribes. → [[OQ-02 Creed to Prescription]]
11. **Commitment may have a second shadow** — insufficient grip, which is Tom's own named weakness. → [[Commitment without Rigidity]]
12. **The framework applies to itself.** Values (radically individual) and duties (radically relational) are themselves a held tension — say so. → [[Part 4 — Duties, the Philosophical Constitution]]
