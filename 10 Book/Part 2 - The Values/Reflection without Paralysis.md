# Reflection without Paralysis

## Definition (Tom, notebook)
> Holding the patient expansive inquiry of the philosopher alongside the urgent, pragmatic delivery of the practitioner. Thought that sharpens action, not thought that substitutes it.

Source: [[Spread E — Reflection, Proactive Reactivity, Tension]]

## Reading
This value is autobiographical: it names the two halves of Tom's working life — philosopher and practitioner — and insists they be held *alongside*, not sequenced. The shadow is the one Tom has named as his achilles heel: the drift between thinking and action without completing either. → [[Dialogue Method]]

## The shadow
**Paralysis** — thought that substitutes for action.

## Relationships
- Explains the *method* of the whole project: philosophy built inductively from practice, with Reyor as the test-bed → [[Inductive Method]]
- The Reyor & H+G working heuristics ("deadline-triggered defaults", "cap active build projects at one", "sort by reversibility") are this value made operational
