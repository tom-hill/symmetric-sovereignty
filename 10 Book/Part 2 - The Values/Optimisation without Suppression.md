# Optimisation without Suppression

## Definition (Tom, notebook)
> Systematising and scaling what works without ever suffocating the chaotic, creative spark that gave it life. Efficiency must serve the human anomaly, never replacing it with a smoother, emptier version of itself.

Source: [[Spread D — Vulnerability, Commitment, Optimisation]]

## Reading
The most directly *anti-normalisation* of the values. "The human anomaly" is the individual — the thing that doesn't fit the system — and the value insists that systematising must *serve* it. "A smoother, emptier version of itself" is the notebook's description of what normalisation produces. → [[Anti-normalisation]], [[Normalisation]]

This is also the value the Reyor positioning was assessed against ("optimisation without suppression, leadership without entitlement, agency without domination") and found strongly coherent. → [[Reyor Positioning]]

## The shadow
**Suppression** — a clear *dosage* shadow: some systematising is the value itself; the failure is progressive. → [[Dosage]], [[OQ-01 Inversion vs Dosage Risks]]
