# Leadership without Entitlement

## Definition (Tom, notebook)
> Taking responsibility for guiding systems and holding the tension between competing forces, without assuming inherent superiority over those within the system or demanding compliance as tax of my position. Authority earned through service, not claimed through rank.

Source: [[Spread C — Strength, Agency, Leadership]]

## Reading

The shadow is not tyranny but **entitlement**: the quieter assumption of superiority, and the demand for compliance "as tax of my position". The closing line is the framework's definition of [[Earned Authority]].
