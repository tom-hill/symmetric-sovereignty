# Agency without Domination

## Definition (Tom, notebook)
> Pursuing absolute sovereignty over your own thinking, works, and systems, while refusing to use that sovereignty to standardise, control, or override the agency of others. The rejection of being controlled must never invert into the impulse to control.

Source: [[Spread C — Strength, Agency, Leadership]]

## Reading
This is the value closest to the name of the whole framework. Sovereignty is claimed *absolutely* — over thinking, works, systems — and in the same breath denied any reach over another. The last sentence is the clearest statement of the inversion mechanism anywhere in the notebook: **the rejection of being controlled must never invert into the impulse to control.** → [[Shadow (Inversion)]]

## The shadow
**Domination** — using one's own sovereignty to standardise, control, or override. Note that *standardise* is listed alongside control and override: normalisation is named as a form of domination. → [[Normalisation]]

## Relationships
- The seed of [[Sovereignty]] — "legitimate only when it preserves others' sovereignty"
- Sibling of [[Strength without Cruelty]] and [[Leadership without Entitlement]] → [[OQ-05 Three Faces or One Value]]
