# Strength without Cruelty

## Definition (Tom, notebook)
> Developing and possessing strength — be that physical, intellectual, economic, or otherwise — but using it exclusively to protect and elevate the strength of others, never to dominate, exploit or force.

Source: [[Spread C — Strength, Agency, Leadership]]

## Reading
The strength is unconditional — *develop and possess it*, in every domain. The constraint is entirely on its *use*. Note the word **exclusively**: strength that is merely *not* cruel is not enough; it must be turned outward, to protect and elevate the strength of *others*. This makes the value relational even though it is self-administered.

## The shadow
**Cruelty** — strength that dominates, exploits, or forces. This reads as an *inversion*: strength does not gradually shade into cruelty; it flips when it stops being for others. → [[Shadow (Inversion)]], [[OQ-01 Inversion vs Dosage Risks]]

## Relationships
- Sibling of [[Agency without Domination]] and [[Leadership without Entitlement]] — Tom asks whether these three are one value → [[OQ-05 Three Faces or One Value]]
- The mechanism preventing the flip is [[Kindness]]
- Applied: the Reyor differentiator — *making change survivable for the humans inside it* — is strength (executive capability) used to protect others through a discontinuity. → [[Reyor]]
