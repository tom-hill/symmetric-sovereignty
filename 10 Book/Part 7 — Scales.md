# Part 7 — Scales

## Material (stated)
- The core principle (health = consciously held tension) extends across scales: **individual, relational, institutional, civilisational**.
- The framework is cautiously moving from personal creed toward broader application. → [[OQ-02 Creed to Prescription]]
- Whether the VC critique targets *mechanism* or *scale* is an open question — i.e. does something go wrong specifically when a good mechanism gets big? → [[OQ-03 VC Critique — Mechanism or Scale]]
- Notebook: [[Optimisation without Suppression]] — "systematising and scaling what works without ever suffocating the chaotic, creative spark that gave it life."

## Argument

> [!blank]
> **What changes as the principle scales, and what must not?** At the individual scale, holding tension is an act of attention. At the relational scale it needs a second sovereign to consent. At the institutional scale it needs *structure* — and structure is exactly the thing that normalises. At the civilisational scale it becomes politics. The chapter probably has to show that the *duties* (Part 4) are what let the principle survive scaling, because values can't be scaled (they're self-administered) but duties can (they bind counterparties).

> [!blank]
> **A worked example per scale.** Individual: a value held under pressure. Relational: a marriage or partnership (the Reyor/H+G structure is a candidate — see [[Reyor and H+G Structure]]). Institutional: a company through a discontinuity (the Reyor ICP). Civilisational: the VC/startup economy (Part 8).

> [!claude]
> The project memory lists "Scale without Structure" among the value formulations. Read literally, that's a *failure* (scaling without the structure to hold it), not a value — unless the intended value is "Structure without Scale" or the pair has been inverted in transcription. Same issue with "Pace without Purpose". → [[OQ-09 Pace and Scale Formulations]]
