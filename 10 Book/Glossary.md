# Glossary

Working definitions, in the framework's own sense. Each links to the concept note where the definition is argued.

- **Tension** — the automatic result of real forces acting on a system; neutral physics; the generative condition of individuality and health. Not conflict. → [[Tension]]
- **Sovereignty** — authority over one's own thinking, work, and systems; legitimate only when it preserves others' sovereignty. → [[Sovereignty]]
- **Symmetric** — **an act, not a state.** The ongoing holding of power such that no party, group or people is overwhelmed and no party can foreclose another. It is in flux and has no universal definition of "state", which is why it is not a [[Resolution Words|resolution word]] despite appearances. → [[OQ-11 The Name]]
- **Value** — an "X without Y" formulation; a strength held against its shadow; self-administered. → [[Values Index]]
- **Duty** — an obligation owed to a counterparty; binding whether or not they hold the philosophy; the duties form the philosophical constitution. → [[Values vs Duties]]
- **Shadow** — what a value inverts into when it stops preserving the other's sovereignty (cruelty, domination, entitlement…). → [[Shadow (Inversion)]]
- **Kindness** — the mechanism that prevents inversion. Its own shadow is condemnation. → [[Kindness]], [[Condemnation]]
- **Normalisation** — the flattening of individuality by society, culture, and business; a dosage problem, not an inversion. → [[Normalisation]]
- **Dosage** — the framing under which a force is judged by whether it adds or displaces, not by whether it is good or bad. → [[Dosage]]
- **Additive / Displacing** — a force that leaves room for other forces vs one that crowds them out. → [[Additive vs Displacing Forces]]
- **Authorship / Imposition** — whether a norm was written by those it governs or applied to them. → [[Authorship vs Imposition]]
- **Reversibility / Exit** — the ability to leave or undo. The most portable *instrument* of power symmetry — it works without requiring the other party's agreement — rather than the foundation itself. → [[Reversibility and Exit]], [[Foreclosure]]
- **Foreclosure** — a norm that can no longer be corrected by the people it is wrong for: not exitable, not revisable, no feedback path. The criterion that replaced "scale". → [[Foreclosure]]
- **Earned authority** — authority through service, not rank. → [[Earned Authority]]
- **Resolution word** — a term that smuggles in a rest state ("balance", "symmetry", "harmony"). Rejected. → [[Resolution Words]]
- **Convergence** — when a principle arrives independently from multiple directions; treated as evidence of structural importance. → [[Convergence as Evidence]]
- **Genuine want** — a want that requires no self-persuasion. → [[Genuine Wants]]
- **Scales** — individual, relational, institutional, civilisational. → [[Scales]]
