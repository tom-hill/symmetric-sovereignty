# Manuscript Map

*The proposed shape of the work. The order is an argument in itself: from the physics of tension, through the individual's values, to the duties owed to others, to the systems (business, institutions) where the philosophy gets tested against people who don't hold it.*

> [!claude]
> This structure is proposed, not settled. It was derived from the pattern in the material: Tom's notebook moves from **quotes → critique of business → values**, and the dialogue sessions moved from **values → duties → reversibility → normalisation-as-dosage → scale**. The map below tries to honour both movements. Reorder freely.
