## The rule that makes this a loop, not an archive
A piece is not finished when it is published. It is finished when whatever came back has been logged as an encounter, and any question it raised exists as an `OQ` or a `UQ`. Otherwise the public layer is a broadcast rather than a test, and the point of publishing was to be tested.

## What is public, and what is not
The vault is private by default; `10 Book`, `20 Open Questions`, `25 Unasked Questions`, `30 Concepts`, `40 Influences`, `15 Published`, `80 Ways of Working` and `Claude/` are promoted to the public repo explicitly. `20` and `25` promote **by default** — the questions are the thing Tom committed to publishing openly, and they should carry the least friction of anything here.

> [!claude]
> The friction is the risk. [[Thread — Outreach Avoidance]] already diagnosed avoidance of publishing, and "not promoted yet" is a comfortable new costume for it. If two sessions pass with nothing in the `published` state, that is the avoidance, not a scheduling problem.
