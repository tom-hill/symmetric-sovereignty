# Bergson

## What he gives the framework
Duration, intuition, the *élan vital* — the idea that life is a creative process that intellect flattens when it stops it to analyse it. Directly behind [[Process Philosophy]] and [[Optimisation without Suppression]] ("the chaotic, creative spark").

## Where he would push back
On systems thinking itself (the MSc): Bergson distrusted the spatialising intellect. A framework that wants to *guide systems* ([[Leadership without Entitlement]]) has to say how it avoids freezing what it guides.
