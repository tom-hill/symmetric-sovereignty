# Marcus Aurelius

## What he gives the framework
The self-administered discipline of the values — Stoic practice as daily, private, and unenforceable on others. The notebook itself is a Stoic form. And the fear question: → [[OQ-06 Fear]] — "do we fear things or is fear the thing to be afraid of?" is a Stoic distinction between the event and the judgement.

## Where he would push back
On [[Tension]] as the goal: the Stoic aim is *ataraxia*, which is close to "standardised stillness". The framework calls that the deeper danger. This is a real disagreement and it deserves a page.
