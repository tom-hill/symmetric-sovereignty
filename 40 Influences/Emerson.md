# Emerson

**Read *Self-Reliance* before almost anything else on the [[Reading List]].**

## What he gives the framework
The anti-normalisation argument, written in 1841 with a clarity the notebook has not yet matched. "Society everywhere is in conspiracy against the manhood of every one of its members." "Whoso would be a man must be a nonconformist." The claim that conformity is not merely limiting but *actively corrosive of the self* is Emerson's, and it is the historical root of [[Anti-normalisation]].

## Where he pushes back
Emerson has no duty layer. Self-reliance extended without a counterparty is precisely the inversion the framework guards against — [[Agency without Domination]] exists to prevent the rejection of control becoming the impulse to control, and Emerson supplies no such brake. Reading him is also a test of [[OQ-14 Normalisation as the Only Unheld Tension]]: Emerson genuinely does treat normalisation as the enemy rather than as a pole to hold, and the framework has to say whether it is doing the same thing.
