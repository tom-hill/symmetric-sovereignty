# Bauhaus

## What it gives the framework
Aesthetics and method: form following function without emptiness; craft and industry held together rather than one displacing the other — [[Optimisation without Suppression]] as a design school. Also the visual language of the Reyor practice (grids, restraint, the Gerstner Programme Grid is a direct descendant) → [[Visual Identity]].

## Where it pushes back
The Bauhaus was itself a normalising force — its style became *the* style. A dosage lesson. → [[Dosage]]
