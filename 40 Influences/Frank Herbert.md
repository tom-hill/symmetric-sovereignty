# Frank Herbert

## What he gives the framework
The most direct influence. Two quotes from *Dune* open the notebook ([[Spread A — Herbert Quotes]]): the warning about turning thinking over to machines, and the process principle that understanding must move with a process rather than stop it. The **Butlerian Jihad** is cited as a reference for process-oriented thinking — a civilisational response to the loss of sovereignty over thought.

## Where he would push back
Herbert distrusted charismatic leaders and any system that asks for faith. A philosophy that names *leadership* among its values would get a hard look — though [[Leadership without Entitlement]] ("earned through service, not claimed through rank") is arguably the Herbertian answer.
