# Nietzsche

## What he gives the framework
The obvious lineage for [[Anti-normalisation]]: the critique of herd values, the suspicion of "peace" as sedation, the insistence that strength is not the same as cruelty (though he is often read otherwise). "Where there is no tension there is no individuality" is a Nietzschean sentence.

## Where he would push back
On [[Kindness]] as the guard mechanism, and on [[Strength without Cruelty]]'s requirement that strength be used *exclusively* for others — he would call that a slave-morality clause. The framework's answer probably runs through [[Sovereignty]]: reciprocity is not weakness, it is the condition of sovereignty at all. Worth arguing explicitly; he is the "necessary challenger" of the reading list.
