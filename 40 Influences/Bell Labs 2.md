# Bell Labs

## What it gives the framework
The counter-example to the VC model: an institution that produced generational, impact-focused work by holding a tension between freedom and structure — researchers given room, inside a company with a purpose. A worked case for [[Optimisation without Suppression]] at institutional scale, and for [[Part 7 — Scales]].

## Where it pushes back
Bell Labs existed because of a monopoly. Held tension at that scale may require a protection the framework would otherwise reject. → [[OQ-03 VC Critique — Mechanism or Scale]]
