# Camus

## What he gives the framework
Revolt without resolution: the absurd is a tension that must not be dissolved by faith or suicide; one lives *in* it. "One must imagine Sisyphus happy" is [[Tension without Immotion]] in one sentence. Also the *rebel*'s limit — revolt that becomes domination has betrayed itself, which is [[Agency without Domination]]'s last sentence.

## Where he would push back
On [[Sovereignty]] as a word — Camus was suspicious of anything that sounded like a claim to rule, even over oneself.
