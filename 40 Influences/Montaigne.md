# Montaigne

## What he gives the framework
The essay as method: reporting from one life, inductively, with the self as the test case, and without pretending to prescribe. The honest form of [[OQ-02 Creed to Prescription]]. Also the notebook habit and the tolerance for contradiction across entries.

## Where he would push back
On the *duties*. Montaigne doubted that one life's findings bind anyone else. If the framework wants a constitution, it leaves Montaigne behind at Part 4 — and should say so.
