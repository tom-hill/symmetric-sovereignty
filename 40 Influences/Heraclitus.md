# Heraclitus

## What he gives the framework
Flux, and the unity of opposites — "the bow and the lyre": harmony that exists *because of* tension, not despite it. Probably the oldest statement of [[Tension]] as generative. Also process: you cannot step in the same river twice → [[Process Philosophy]].

## Where he would push back
Heraclitus' harmony is a *hidden* harmony — he might say the framework's refusal of the word "balance" is a quarrel with vocabulary, not with him. → [[Resolution Words]], [[OQ-11 The Name]]
