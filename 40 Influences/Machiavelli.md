# Machiavelli

**Read the *Discourses on Livy* alongside *The Prince*, not instead of it.**

## What he gives the framework
Everyone reads *The Prince* and thinks they know Machiavelli. The *Discourses* carry the real argument: **republican virtue, earned legitimacy, and what makes leadership sustainable** rather than merely effective. That is [[Earned Authority]] and [[Leadership without Entitlement]] with two thousand years of worked examples attached.

He also supplies something the framework badly needs: a theory of **institutional design under the assumption that people will not be good**. Machiavelli's republics work because of structural checks, not shared values — which is [[Restraint in Machinery]] argued by someone with no illusions.

## Where he pushes back
Directly at [[OQ-15 The Hidden Premise About Human Nature]]. Machiavelli assumes self-interest is durable and designs around it. The framework hopes the right structure releases something cooperative underneath. If Machiavelli is right, the hope is decorative and only the machinery matters.
