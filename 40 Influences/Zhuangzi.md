# Zhuangzi

## What he gives the framework
Process philosophy **lived rather than argued** — the other great Daoist text, more playful than [[Lao Tzu]], built from stories and paradoxes rather than propositions. The butterfly dream: am I a man dreaming I am a butterfly, or a butterfly dreaming I am a man?

The relevance is formal as much as substantive. Zhuangzi demonstrates that a philosophy of process can be *transmitted* without being systematised — which is a live problem for a framework that wants to resist being finished. → [[Part 10 — Process and the Machine]]

## Where he pushes back
He is the clearest instance of the third blind spot in [[OQ-16 Morality Across Scales and the Blind Side]]: moral and philosophical insight encoded in a form that resists extraction into principles. Any summary of Zhuangzi is worse than Zhuangzi. If that is true of him, it may be true of things this vault is trying to capture — and the vault's method would be the problem, not the subject.
